## What's New in 2.0.1

- **Your settings are always saved,** even a change made just before closing the studio.

## Key Features

- Gets text from the page you're on, or works with text you paste in
- Lets you choose the colours, shape, background, and number of words
- Saves your word cloud as a PNG image

## How to Use

1. Click the Word Cloud icon in your toolbar:
   - **Get text from this page** opens the studio with the text of the page you're on.
   - **Open the studio** opens it empty, ready for you to paste your own text.
2. Choose a colour scheme, word limit, shape, and background, then click **Generate word cloud**.
3. In the word list, click × to remove a word you don't want.
4. Click **Save as PNG** to download your word cloud.

## Privacy

We do not collect any user data. Nothing you type is ever sent to us. Your settings and excluded words are stored by your browser and synced through your browser account.

Full details: [privacy policy](PRIVACY.md).

## Compatibility

Compatible with most Chromium-based browsers, including Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge and Vivaldi.
